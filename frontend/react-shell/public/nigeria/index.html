Buy and sell coins in naira

Verified coin traders publish buy and sell desks for GTEX Coin and Fan Coin. Discovery can be filtered to Nigeria. Orders use canonical escrow. Naira methods include bank transfer, OPay, PalmPay, and Kuda. Traders complete identity checks, including BVN or NIN where that lane applies.

Club creation and the 3D operations centre

A new club starts in foundation. Money does not choose the division. The operations centre is the daily 3D desk for staff, facilities, and matchday decisions. League entry is a right of ownership, not a purchase.

Academy pipeline and Build a Son

Youth intake feeds the academy. Build a Son keeps the parent's surname, position, foot, and DNA unless you pay to change them. The heir joins the reserve squad of the club you have open.

National team competitions

Club competitions and national-team competitions are free to enter. For a national team, the squad rental is the only cost. Prize coin is funded by GTEX.

Player purchase and sales

Active players trade as share markets. Legends are one of one, shown as ICON · 1 OF 1, and no further shares are minted after a purchase. Archived duplicates stay off the market.

Questions
