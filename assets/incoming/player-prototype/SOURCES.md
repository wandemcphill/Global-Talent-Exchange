# GTEX home-kit player

Local source only. The GLB and PNGs stay on this machine. The match loads the repainted public copy.

- Mesh: the purchased Mixamo-rigged soccer player, 65 bones
- File: `gtex_home_kit_mixamo.glb`
- Textures: `textures/head.png`, `shirt.png`, `shorts.png`, `hands.png`, `legs.png`
- Kit: deep green `#104A3A`, gold `#D4A84A`, sand `#ECE0C4`
- The photographic face, chest badge, squad number, and sock mark are painted out
- The public kit replaces the baked likeness and crest with a generic face, white cloth, and a separate G badge
- Club color tints the white shirt at runtime
- Real club kits and a real likeness stay out of the product
