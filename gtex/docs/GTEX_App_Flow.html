Step 6 — Build-a-Son

Create and develop next-generation players

Managers pay a creation fee to build a regen player who inherits traits from a real or existing squad player. Attributes are backend-derived based on lineage, position, and a randomised development curve. Regens mature over seasons — creating a long-term emotional attachment to your club.

Operator queues, approvals, and platform health
