The problem GTEX solves
Football discovery, finance, and operations are broken across siloed products. GTEX replaces all of them with a single auditable system.

Fragmented football economy

Scouting, wallets, content, competitions, and admin actions live in disconnected tools with no common source of truth or cross-product audit trail.

Opaque finance

Wallet balances, reserved funds, transfer bids, and creator payouts are hard to explain and easy to dispute without a backend-authored ledger layer.

Weak retention loops

Football apps often lack a daily operating loop beyond score-watching. Engagement drops between fixtures, seasons, and transfer windows.

Manual ops overhead

Approvals, dispute resolution, settlement, and export reconciliation consume staff time that should be automated by queues and audit trails.

GTEX answer: one backend-authored system where every event — bid, payout, match tick, creator post — produces auditable state. Ops teams get queues, not spreadsheets. Users get real-time truth.
